The Dwell Method

Building blocks for biblical literacy.

Because quick answers don't produce deep lives. Dwell gives your eyes and mind a specific job through five intentional read-throughs of Scripture:

  • A repeatable rhythm to quiet distractions and slow down.
  • Modular blocks you can practice one-by-one or stack together.
Begin with Discover
D Discover
Step One • Discover

Ground the passage in its true setting.

  • Identify author • time frame • audience • purpose.
  • Put away commentaries for now and read slowly to note characters and setting.
Advance to Word Work
W Word Work
Step Two • Word Work

Zoom into the structure and words.

  • Look for repeated words, lists, comparisons, and structural emphasis.
  • Look up unfamiliar or theological terms for clarity.
  • Summarize the whole text in 1–2 precise sentences.
Advance to Expand
E Expand
Step Three • Expand

Connect the passage to the larger story.

  • Compare multiple translations to clarify confusing phrases.
  • Read the surrounding chapters to see the broader literary arc.
  • Follow 1–2 cross-references into scripture.
Advance to Learn
L Learn
Step Four • Learn

Cross the bridge to eternal truth.

  • How would the original audience have understood this?
  • What does this reveal about God's unchanging character?
  • Verify your conclusions with others in community.
Advance to Live
L Live
Step Five • Live

Let truth transform daily life.

  • What cultural or personal lies are challenged by this truth?
  • How will this shape your prayers, words, and actions today?
