The Dwell Method

Building blocks for biblical literacy[cite: 1].

Because quick answers don't produce deep lives[cite: 1]. Dwell gives your eyes and mind a specific job through five intentional read-throughs of Scripture[cite: 1]:

  • A repeatable rhythm to quiet distractions and slow down[cite: 1].
  • Modular blocks you can practice one-by-one or stack together[cite: 1].
Study Guides
D
Discover
Step One • Discover

Ground the passage in its true setting.

  • Identify author • time frame • audience • purpose[cite: 1].
  • Put away commentaries for now and read slowly to note characters and setting[cite: 1].
W
Word Work
Step Two • Word Work

Zoom into the structure and words.

  • Look for repeated words, lists, comparisons, and structural emphasis[cite: 1].
  • Look up unfamiliar or theological terms for clarity[cite: 1].
  • Summarize the whole text in 1–2 precise sentences[cite: 1].
E
Expand
Step Three • Expand

Connect the passage to the larger story.

  • Compare multiple translations to clarify confusing phrases[cite: 1].
  • Read the surrounding chapters to see the broader literary arc[cite: 1].
  • Follow 1–2 cross-references into scripture[cite: 1].
L
Learn
Step Four • Learn

Cross the bridge to eternal truth.

  • How would the original audience have understood this?[cite: 1]
  • What does this reveal about God's unchanging character?[cite: 1]
  • Verify your conclusions with others in community[cite: 1].
L
Live
Step Five • Live

Let truth transform daily life.

  • What cultural or personal lies are challenged by this truth?[cite: 1]
  • How will this shape your prayers, words, and actions today?[cite: 1]
